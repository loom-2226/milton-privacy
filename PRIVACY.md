# MILTON Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 19 November 2026

MILTON is a privately operated personal-finance application for its authorised account holder, not a public banking service.

## Data collected and purposes
With the account holder's explicit Openfeed consent, MILTON may access selected banking account identifiers, account descriptions, balances, transactions, dates, amounts, merchant descriptions, currencies, and consent metadata. The account holder may also provide statements manually. This information is used for personal financial reconciliation, categorisation, budgeting and reporting. MILTON does not initiate payments.

## Storage and protection
MILTON is designed to keep financial records in a privately controlled AWS-hosted environment using an encrypted ledger and restricted access to files and cryptographic keys. Openfeed and participating banks process information to deliver the authorised banking connection under their respective privacy practices. Independent off-server backups are planned but are not yet represented as operational.

## Disclosure and AI
MILTON does not sell banking information or use it for advertising. Its intended AI architecture processes sensitive records locally and permits only restricted, code-filtered summaries to external AI services. Raw bank transactions are not intended to be submitted to external generative AI services. Infrastructure and connectivity providers may process information necessary to operate their services.

## Consent and withdrawal
The account holder chooses which eligible accounts to share and may revoke consent through Openfeed or their bank's data-sharing controls. Revocation stops future authorised collection but does not automatically delete information already stored locally.

## Retention and deletion
Records are kept as long as the account holder needs them for personal financial tracking and reconciliation. The account holder can delete local data, subject to recovery copies and applicable obligations. Future backups will have separate retention and recovery procedures.

## Accuracy
Bank feeds can be delayed, incomplete or corrected. MILTON is not a bank, does not move money, and does not provide regulated financial advice.

## Changes and enquiries
This policy may be updated when data flows or operating practices change. Privacy enquiries can be directed to the application operator via the [public policy repository](https://github.com/loom-2226/milton-privacy). Do not post account details, passwords, identity documents or transaction records in public GitHub issues.
